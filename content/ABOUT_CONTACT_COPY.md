## About — /about

**Where this came from**

We're RVers who got tired of being the product. The parks game the
reviews. The lead farms sell your number. The apps we loved got
bought, paywalled, or shut down. So we're building the thing we
wanted: a home base where reviews are hard to fake and hard to bury,
and where the mechanic, the tow operator, and the storage yard sit in
the same room as the campground — same trust, same scoreboard.

**Who's behind it**

Right now, that's one founder and a growing pile of AI collaborators,
building in the open. Nationwide is the destination. Density is the
method — we start on one corridor (I-15) and earn our way outward.

**How to get involved**

- Join the waitlist — we'll email you when we go live in your
  corridor.
- Are you a provider? Tell us what you do at /provide. Consented
  leads, no shared dumps.
- Know a place worth listing? Send it our way.
- Watch the work — we build in the open on GitHub.

**What we are, plainly**

Not a shop. Not a tow company. Not an insurer. When we match you with
someone, we say so out loud. You hire them. You pay them. You review
them. We're the switchboard and the scoreboard.
