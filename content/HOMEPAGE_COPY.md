## Section: What We're Building (four cards)

**Places**
Verified reviews of parks, campgrounds, and boondocking spots. Rated
on big-rig access, cell signal, safety, and value — the things that
actually matter.

**Help**
Mobile repair, towing, storage, detailing. Same review system as the
parks. A "get help now" form that routes to real humans, not a lead
farm.

**Answers** (badge: Coming later)
Ask the real questions — routes, rigs, what breaks. The best answer
rises on votes instead of getting buried.

**People** (badge: Coming later)
Opt-in, privacy-first. Find your people on the road without becoming
somebody's product.
