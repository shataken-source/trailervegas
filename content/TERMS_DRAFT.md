## What TrailerVegas is

TrailerVegas is a directory and a routing service. We help you find RV
parks, campgrounds, and service providers, and we connect help
requests to real humans. We are the switchboard and the scoreboard —
not the service.

---

## What TrailerVegas is not

We are not a repair shop, a tow company, a storage operator, or an
insurer. We do not fix your rig, tow it, store it, or cover it. When
we match you with a provider, they are an independent business. You
hire them. You pay them. You review them. Any agreement is between
you and them.

## Reviews and content

Reviews and photos are user-generated. We moderate for spam and abuse
— not for disagreement. Money can't buy a star: ads are ads, rankings
are rankings. Your reviews and photos are yours; you'll be able to
export them.
