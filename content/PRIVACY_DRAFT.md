## The short version

We collect the least we can, we use it only to do what you asked, and
we will never sell your personal data. That last part isn't marketing
— it's in our Trust Covenant.

---

## What we collect

- **Waitlist:** your email.
- **Help requests:** name, email, phone, location, rig type, problem
  type, and anything you write in the description.
- **Provider applications:** business name, contact name, email,
  phone, website, service types, service area, and the details you
  share.
- **Automatically, for every form:** your IP address and browser
  user-agent, stored as a security and consent record.

---

## Why we collect it

- To route your help request to a nearby provider.
- To email you waitlist updates when we launch in your corridor.
- To review and contact providers who apply.
- To keep a consent record (which version of our Trust Covenant you
  saw) and to fight spam and abuse.

---

## Who we share it with

**Help requests only:** we share your contact and problem details with
the specific provider(s) we match you to, so they can give you a
quote. That's the whole point of the form, and we tell you before you
submit.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data
brokers. Ever.

---

## Analytics

We use Plausible — privacy-first, cookieless, and it does not track
you across other sites. No Google Analytics.

---

## Cookies

Phase 0 sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Only what's strictly
needed to make the site work.

---

## How to delete your data

Email us and we'll remove your information:
support@trailervegas.com. No account required, no hoops.
