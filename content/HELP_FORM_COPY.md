## Above the fold

**Headline:** RV down? Get a local who actually works on rigs.

**Subhead:** Tell us what's wrong and where you are. We'll connect you
with a vetted mobile tech, tow operator, or storage yard near you — a
real human, not a lead farm.

---

## Field labels & helper text

- **Name** — "Your name" (required)
- **Email** — "Where we send your confirmation" (required)
- **Phone** — "How the provider reaches you" (required)
- **Location** — "City, park name, or mile marker — whatever you've
  got" (required)
- **Rig type** — dropdown (required): Class A, Class B, Class C,
  Travel Trailer, 5th Wheel, Toy Hauler, Campervan, Skoolie, Truck
  Camper, Other
- **Problem type** — dropdown (required): Engine, Electrical,
  Plumbing, AC/Heating, Slide-out, Tire, Towing, Storage, Other
- **Urgency** — radio (required): Emergency (need help now) ·
  Scheduled (this week) · Planning (flexible)
- **Description** — "Anything that helps a tech show up ready.
  Optional." (optional)
- **Consent checkbox** (required): "I agree to be contacted by local
  service providers, and I understand my info may be shared with them
  so they can give me a quote."
- **Submit button:** Send my request

---

## Validation microcopy

- Missing required field: "We need this one to route your request."
- Bad email: "That email doesn't look right — check it?"
- Bad phone: "We need a number a provider can actually call."
- Consent unchecked: "We can't route your request without this."
- Rate limited (429): "You've sent a few already. Give it an hour and
  try again."
- Server error (500): "Something broke on our end. Try again in a
  minute — your info wasn't lost."
