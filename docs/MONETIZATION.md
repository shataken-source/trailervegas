# Monetization

**Last updated:** 2026-09-27
**Maintained by:** Claude

---

## The Principle

The incumbents' biggest sin is nickel-and-diming users. Our monetization
should feel fair.

If we ever charge for something, cancellation is boring and easy.
If we ever run ads, they're labeled.
If we ever sell leads, they're qualified and consented.

---

## Year One (No Audience Yet)

These work with zero liquidity:

### 1. Disclosed Affiliate Links

- Amazon Associates (RV gear, solar, camping)
- RVshare / Outdoorsy (rental bookings)
- Camping World / Walmart
- Clearly disclosed. Never affects rankings.

### 2. Merch

- "Trailer Vegas" brand is inherently merchandisable
- Stickers, shirts, hats, mugs
- Printful + Etsy or Shopify
- Launch with 3–5 designs (see MERCH_IDEAS.md if/when created)

### 3. Featured Listings (Clearly Labeled)

- Parks and providers can pay for prominent placement
- Always labeled "Sponsored"
- Never affects reviews
- $29–$79/month

---

## Year Two (Density Exists)

### 4. Qualified Lead Fees

- Service providers pay per qualified, consented lead
- Not a phone dump. Not a blast list.
- One or two providers get the job details
- They pay only if they want more
- Pricing: $10–$75 per lead depending on category

### 5. Booking Commissions

- Campground booking through our link
- Small commission
- Never costs the camper extra

### 6. Pro Features (Optional)

- Offline maps
- Advanced filters
- Unlimited trip planning
- $30–40/year, not $60+
- Cancel in three clicks

---

## Never

- Selling user data
- Pay-to-play rankings
- Auto-renew traps
- Charging for basic reviews or search
- Selling raw phone dumps

---

## The Public Commitment

Whatever we charge for, the Trust Covenant says:

- Clear renewal notice
- One-click cancel
- No dark patterns
- No retention specialists
- No phone calls to cancel

This is the differentiator. It costs nothing to build. It costs
incumbents a lot to fix.
