# TrailerVegas Trust Covenant

**Version 1.2 — Published 2026-09-27**
**This document is public. It is linked in the footer of every page.**
**It is versioned. Changes are logged.**

---

## What This Is

TrailerVegas is a nationwide home base for RV families. This covenant
explains who owns it, who will own it, and what will never change.

It exists because the RV community has been burned before — by
platforms that promised community and delivered extraction.

We're not asking you to trust us. We're showing you the contract.

---

## 1. Who Owns TrailerVegas Today

TrailerVegas is currently owned and operated by **Jason Cochran**, as a
**Delaware limited liability company (LLC)**.

It is not owned by a conglomerate. It is not owned by a private equity
firm. It is not owned by Thor Industries, Roadpass Digital, or any of
their subsidiaries.

It is one person, for now, on purpose. This is the fastest way to build
the thing. It is not the final structure.

---

## 2. Who Will Own TrailerVegas Later

TrailerVegas will convert to a **community-owned cooperative** upon
reaching the first of the following triggers:

- **50,000 registered users** with at least one Verified Contribution
  (as defined in Article X of the operating agreement), **or**
- **$500,000 in cumulative consolidated gross revenue**, **or**
- **December 31, 2029** — whichever comes first.

When that happens:

- **Membership is free.** Every registered user in good standing
  becomes a member.
- **One member, one vote.** Governance is democratic. Not by
  investment. Not by tenure. Not by rig size.
- **Surplus is distributed by usage.** Reviewer pool (60%) and payer
  pool (40%). Percentages locked in Exhibit A.
- **The board is elected.** Members elect the board. The board hires
  and fires the CEO. The founder becomes an employee or board member,
  not an owner.

**Legal fallback:** If a direct cooperative conversion is legally
impossible in the chosen state, a member-owned benefit corporation is
permitted **only if** it carries all four non-negotiable attributes:

1. One-member-one-vote on ranking, ad, and data policy.
2. No investor veto over conversion.
3. Patronage distribution or mission lock.
4. Founder's economic interest capped by a published formula.

If any attribute is missing, the fallback is void.

The full conversion plan is in **Exhibit A**. It is public. Version
0.1 is published at `docs/EXHIBIT_A_STUB.md`. The full draft is due
2026-10-15.

## 3. What Will Never Change

These commitments survive conversion. They are written into the
operating agreement. Changing them requires a **two-thirds member
vote** (once members exist) or **the founder's written consent plus a
two-thirds vote of the Advisory Council plus a 90-day public notice
period** (before members exist). A comment period is not a veto. The
Council vote is.

### We will never sell your personal data.

Not to advertisers. Not to data brokers. Not to "partners." Not ever.
Your email, your location, your rig info, your reviews — they belong to
you.

If we ever share data with a third party, it is because you explicitly
asked us to, and you can revoke that at any time.

**Exception that is not an exception:** If you submit a help request,
we send the details you typed to the providers we match you with.
Name, phone, location, rig, problem. That is the job. You can refuse
to submit. You can ask us to delete the request after it's matched. We
do not resell the list. This is said in the form, the confirmation
email, and the provider email. It is not hidden.

### Ads are ads. Rankings are rankings.

Businesses can buy ads. Businesses can sponsor content. But no amount
of money changes what the community says about them.

Paid placement is always labeled. Review rankings are never for sale.
The algorithm is documented publicly.

### Your contributions leave with you.

You can export your reviews, photos, lists, and profile data at any
time, in a standard format.

If you leave, you leave with everything you brought. We will never hold
your data hostage.

### Cancellation will be boring and easy.

If we ever charge for anything, canceling will take fewer than three
clicks. No phone calls. No retention specialists. No dark patterns. No
auto-renew without a clear reminder.

The incumbents taught the RV community to flinch at subscriptions.
We're not doing that.

### We will not become the thing we hate.

If TrailerVegas is ever acquired, the acquirer must agree in writing
to assume Article X of the operating agreement, or the deal does not
happen. Asset sales, exclusive brand licenses, and acqui-hires that
move community data without assuming Article X are banned. The board
has no discretion to override this.

If we ever take outside investment, the investors must agree to the
conversion plan before the money hits the account. The operating
agreement says so.

## 4. What "Community-Governed" Means Before Conversion

We don't have members yet. We don't have a co-op yet. But we're not
waiting to give the community a voice.

### The Advisory Council (nominated 2026-09-27)

The initial Council nominating pool:

Council candidates have been nominated. Names will be published
after the public comment window opens and candidates confirm they
consent to being named.

A public 14-day comment window opens when Phase 0 launches. Following
comment, 3–5 of the pool will be confirmed as the seated Council.
Terms are 12 months, maximum 2 consecutive.

The Council has a **veto** over:

- Changes to the ranking algorithm.
- Changes to the data policy.
- Changes to the ad policy.
- Any amendment to Article X before members exist.

That veto is in writing. It is in Article X.3 of the operating
agreement.

### Other commitments between now and conversion:

- **The roadmap is public.** Feature requests are visible and
  upvotable.
- **The moderation log is public.** Reason codes and counts are
  published. Individual identities are protected until a formal
  process exists.
- **Quarterly state of the union.** Progress toward conversion. Three
  numbers every quarter: gross receipts vs $500k, users vs 50k, and
  whether any affiliate received platform-related revenue.
- **The founder is the moderator for now.** That's honest. The Council
  is the check on that power until members exist.

## 5. What TrailerVegas Is Not

- **We are not a shop.** We don't fix RVs.
- **We are not a tow company.** We don't hook up your rig.
- **We are not an insurer.** We don't cover your damages.
- **We are not a campground.** We don't own the land.

We are the switchboard and the scoreboard. When we connect you with a
service provider, we say that out loud. You hire them. You pay them.
You review them. We route the request and we publish the reviews.
That's it.

## 6. How We Make Money

This is the part incumbents hide. We're putting it on the front page.

### Today:

- **Disclosed affiliate links.** If you buy gear through our links, we
  get a small commission. It never affects rankings.
- **Merch.** Stickers, shirts, hats. The "Trailer Vegas" brand is fun.
  We sell it. You can buy it.
- **Featured listings (clearly labeled).** Parks and service providers
  can pay for prominent placement. Always labeled "Sponsored." Never
  affects reviews.

### Later (once density exists):

- **Qualified lead fees.** Service providers pay per qualified,
  consented lead. Not a phone dump. Not a blast list. One or two
  providers get the job details. They pay only if they want more. The
  user sees the consent screen before submission.
- **Booking commissions.** If you book a campground through our link,
  we get a small commission. It never costs you extra.
- **Pro features (optional).** Offline maps. Advanced filters.
  Unlimited trip planning. $30–40/year, not $60+. Cancel in three
  clicks.

### Never:

- Selling user data.
- Pay-to-play rankings.
- Auto-renew traps.
- Charging for basic reviews or search.

## 7. The Promise, in Plain English

We're building this because the platforms we loved got ruined.

We're starting as a Delaware LLC because that's the fastest way to
build.

We're committing to convert to a co-op because that's the right way to
end.

If we hit the trigger and don't convert, you'll know. The operating
agreement is public. The conversion plan (Exhibit A) is public. The
quarterly updates are public.

If we break this promise, it will be the last thing we ever do,
because the RV community will never trust us again.

We'd rather earn your trust by showing you the contract than by asking
you to believe us.
