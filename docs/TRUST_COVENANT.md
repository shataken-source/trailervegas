# TrailerVegas Trust Covenant

**Version 1.0 — Published 2026-09-27**
**This document is public. It is linked in the footer of every page.**
**It is versioned. Changes are logged.**

---

## What This Is

TrailerVegas is a nationwide home base for RV families. This covenant
explains who owns it, who will own it, and what will never change.

It exists because the RV community has been burned before — by platforms
that promised community and delivered extraction.

We're not asking you to trust us. We're showing you the contract.

---

## 1. Who Owns TrailerVegas Today

TrailerVegas is currently owned and operated by **Jason Cochran**, as a
limited liability company (LLC).

It is not owned by a conglomerate. It is not owned by a private equity
firm. It is not owned by Thor Industries, Roadpass Digital, or any of
their subsidiaries.

It is one person, for now, on purpose. This is the fastest way to build
the thing. It is not the final structure.

---

## 2. Who Will Own TrailerVegas Later

TrailerVegas will convert to a **community-owned cooperative** (or a
member-owned benefit corporation, depending on what our lawyers and the
community decide is best) upon reaching **the first of the following
triggers**:

- **50,000 registered users** with at least one verified contribution
  (review, listing claim, answer, or help request), **or**
- **$500,000 in cumulative gross revenue**, **or**
- **December 31, 2029** — whichever comes first.

When that happens:

- **Membership is free.** Every registered user in good standing becomes
  a member.
- **One member, one vote.** Governance is democratic. Not by investment.
  Not by tenure. Not by rig size.
- **Surplus is distributed by usage.** If TrailerVegas generates surplus,
  it goes back to members based on how much they use the platform — not
  how much they put in.
- **The board is elected.** Members elect the board. The board hires and
  fires the CEO. The founder becomes an employee or board member, not an
  owner.

The full conversion plan is attached as **Exhibit A** to our operating
agreement. It is public. It will be linked here once drafted.

---

## 3. What Will Never Change

These are the commitments that survive conversion. They are written into
the operating agreement. Changing them requires a **supermajority vote of
members** (once members exist) or **the founder's written consent plus a
90-day public comment period** (before members exist).

### We will never sell your personal data.

Not to advertisers. Not to data brokers. Not to "partners." Not ever.
Your email, your location, your rig info, your reviews — they belong to
you.

If we ever share data with a third party, it is because you explicitly
asked us to, and you can revoke that at any time.

### Ads are ads. Rankings are rankings.

Businesses can buy ads. Businesses can sponsor content. But no amount of
money changes what the community says about them.

Paid placement is always labeled. Review rankings are never for sale.
The algorithm is documented publicly.

### Your contributions leave with you.

You can export your reviews, photos, lists, and profile data at any time,
in a standard format.

If you leave, you leave with everything you brought. We will never hold
your data hostage.

### Cancellation will be boring and easy.

If we ever charge for anything, canceling will take fewer than three
clicks. No phone calls. No retention specialists. No dark patterns. No
auto-renew without a clear reminder.

The incumbents taught the RV community to flinch at subscriptions. We're
not doing that.

### We will not become the thing we hate.

If TrailerVegas is ever acquired, the acquirer must agree to these terms
in writing, or the deal does not happen.

If we ever take outside investment, the investors must agree to the
conversion plan before the money hits the account. The operating
agreement says so.

## 4. What "Community-Governed" Means Before Conversion

We don't have members yet. We don't have a co-op yet. But we're not
waiting to give the community a voice.

### Between now and conversion:

- **The roadmap is public.** Feature requests are visible and upvotable.
- **The moderation log is public.** Every removed review, every banned
  account, with a reason.
- **The advisory council has real teeth.** Five to seven community
  members, selected from active users. They have a **veto** over:
  changes to the ranking algorithm, changes to the data policy, and
  changes to the ad policy. That veto is in writing.
- **Quarterly state of the union.** Progress toward conversion. Rough
  financials. What's working. What's not. Published publicly.
- **The founder is the moderator for now.** That's honest. There's no
  community to elect moderators from yet. The advisory council is the
  check on that power until there is.

---

## 5. What TrailerVegas Is Not

- **We are not a shop.** We don't fix RVs.
- **We are not a tow company.** We don't hook up your rig.
- **We are not an insurer.** We don't cover your damages.
- **We are not a campground.** We don't own the land.

We are the switchboard and the scoreboard. When we connect you with a
service provider, we say that out loud. You hire them. You pay them. You
review them. We route the request and we publish the reviews. That's it.

---

## 6. How We Make Money

This is the part incumbents hide. We're putting it on the front page.

### Today:

- **Disclosed affiliate links.** If you buy gear through our links, we
  get a small commission. It never affects rankings.
- **Merch.** Stickers, shirts, hats. The "Trailer Vegas" brand is fun. We
  sell it. You can buy it.
- **Featured listings (clearly labeled).** Parks and service providers
  can pay for prominent placement. It is always labeled "Sponsored." It
  never affects reviews.

### Later (once density exists):

- **Qualified lead fees.** Service providers pay per qualified, consented
  lead. Not a phone dump. Not a blast list. One or two providers get the
  job details. They pay only if they want more.
- **Booking commissions.** If you book a campground through our link, we
  get a small commission. It never costs you extra.
- **Pro features (optional).** Offline maps. Advanced filters. Unlimited
  trip planning. $30–40/year, not $60+. Cancel in three clicks.

### Never:

- Selling user data.
- Pay-to-play rankings.
- Auto-renew traps.
- Charging for basic reviews or search.

---

## 7. The Promise, in Plain English

We're building this because the platforms we loved got ruined.

We're starting as an LLC because that's the fastest way to build.

We're committing to convert to a co-op because that's the right way to
end.

If we hit the trigger and don't convert, you'll know. The operating
agreement is public. The conversion plan is public. The quarterly updates
are public.

If we break this promise, it will be the last thing we ever do, because
the RV community will never trust us again.

We'd rather earn your trust by showing you the contract than by asking
you to believe us.
