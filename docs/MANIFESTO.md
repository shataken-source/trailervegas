# The TrailerVegas Manifesto

**Version:** 1.0
**Published:** 2026-09-27

---

## We're the RVers who got tired of being the product.

You know the feeling.

You pull into a park that looked great online. Shade. Space. A fire ring.
What you got was a gravel pad, a dead hookup, and a generator at 2 a.m.
You try to leave an honest review. The park reports it. The platform buries
it. Or it wants Pro money before you can say anything useful.

You break down two exits from nowhere. You search "mobile RV repair near
me." Half the results are lead farms. Your number gets sold. Two shops
call. One shows up three days later and prices you like a tourist who
can't leave.

You ask a simple question. "Best route from Flagstaff to Durango with a
40-foot fifth wheel?" Twelve replies. Four help. Three are ads. The good
answer is buried.

You had a favorite app once. Maybe Campendium. Maybe RVillage. You told
your friends. Then a company bought it, redesigned it, paywalled it, or
shut the door. "Join our Facebook group," they said.

---

## The community is better than the platforms.

RVillage was the closest thing to a real RV social network. It was sold
into the Thor / Togo / Roadpass world and closed in March 2023. Users got
pointed to Facebook.

Campendium used to be the clean, RV-first brain a lot of people trusted.
After the Roadtrippers era, long-time users called the new interface a
mess, said maps vanished, and said the product stopped feeling built for
RVers.

RV LIFE still has the biggest campground and trip-planning footprint.
People also keep complaining about routing, crashes, subscription
friction, and GPS you cannot blindly trust.

The Dyrt has a huge campsite pile. Billing and cancellation complaints
are still showing up in 2026.

That is the opening. Not "we have more pins." The incumbents keep teaching
people not to trust the house.

---

## TrailerVegas is a nationwide home base for RV families.

Find the place. Find the wrench. Find the honest answer. Leave the next
person a better map than you had.

Parks. Boondocking. Dump stations. Storage. Mobile repair. Towing.
Propane. Parts. Rentals. Dealers that actually service what they sell.

---

## What we believe

**Reviews should be hard to fake and hard to bury.**
Rate the things that matter: big-rig access, hookups, cell, noise,
safety, value, "would I use them again."

**Mechanics, tow operators, and storage yards belong in the same room as
campgrounds.**
Same review system. Same trust.

**The best answer rises.**
Votes, not thread burial. Filter by rig type.

**Ads are ads. Rankings are rankings.**
Money cannot buy a star.

**Your reviews and photos leave with you.**
Export everything. Anytime. Standard format.

**Start useful, not giant.**
Nationwide is the destination. Density is the method.

**Fun is allowed. Sloppy information is not.**
We're called TrailerVegas. We take the work seriously, not ourselves.

---

## What we are not

Not a shop. Not a tow company. Not an insurer.

When we match you, we say that out loud. You hire them. You pay them. You
review them. We are the switchboard and the scoreboard.

---

## The name

"Nashvegas" is a nickname for a feeling, not a city limit. TrailerVegas
can mean "this is the place RVers actually use," as long as the homepage
says that in the first screen.

Vegas can be the first dense corridor. It cannot be the cage.
