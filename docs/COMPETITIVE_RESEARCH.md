## The One Company You're Actually Up Against

RVillage, Campendium, and Roadtrippers are not three separate cautionary
tales. They are one company: **Thor Industries**, via **Togo Group** and
**Roadpass Digital**.

- RVillage: acquired 2020, shut down March 2023
- Campendium: acquired, redesigned, long-time users revolted
- Roadtrippers: still running, billing complaints persist
- Togo RV: still running

The pattern: acquire, harvest emails, redesign for broader reach, sunset
when it stops being useful.

This is the moat. They can't publish a Trust Covenant because their cap
table won't allow it.

## The Real Trust Gap

It's not "reviews are fake." It's **"nobody will let me stop paying them."**

The Dyrt's 2.6 Trustpilot score is built on just 4 reviews. Its App Store
rating is 4.8 across 113,000. That's not a contradiction — it's a
diagnosis. The product is fine. The billing flow is predatory.

RV LIFE and Roadtrippers: auto-renewed without notice, no cancel button,
no human to dispute with.

**This is fixable in a weekend.** Clear renewal notice. One-click cancel.
No dark patterns. It costs nothing to build and costs incumbents a lot to
fix because it's tangled up in their revenue ops.

## The Missing Product: RV Reliability Database

Nobody has built the **CarComplaints.com of RVs**. A crowdsourced,
model-and-year-specific reliability database:

- Which slide-out fails on which model year
- Which fridge is garbage
- Which floorplan has known frame issues
- Which manufacturer cuts corners where

RV industry executives themselves now say buyers are demanding this
transparency.

Pure content. No marketplace liquidity required. Strong SEO wedge.

## The Mobile Tech Gap

Finding a mobile RV tech is still solved by word of mouth and scattered
forum threads. One RVer described using the Jayco Owners Forum's tech
list specifically because every entry was from an actual owner, not a
directory.

This is the "unclaimed directory" model:

1. Crawl RVIA/NRVA-certified techs + forum-recommended names
2. Create unclaimed profile cards (facts + link out, no fake leads)
3. One-at-a-time outreach: "Is this you? Claim your profile."

## The Harvest Hosts Lesson

Acquisitions kill products when the acquirer redesigns for broader reach
instead of leaving the niche alone.

Harvest Hosts acquired Boondockers Welcome and kept both operating
independently with separate plans. Result: 4.9 stars, 64k ratings, real
love.

The lesson: **stay focused, don't merge into mush.**

## What This Means for TrailerVegas

1. The moat is trust, not features. Incumbents can't copy the Trust
   Covenant without blowing up their own cap table.
2. The billing flow is the cheapest trust win. Build it right from day
   one.
3. The reliability database is a real SEO wedge that doesn't require
   marketplace liquidity.
4. The "unclaimed directory" model lets us seed listings without faking
   data or spamming providers.
5. Stay focused. Don't merge into mush.
