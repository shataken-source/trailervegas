**Finding 3 — No standing until Jason builds the jury.**
Three moves:
1. Name the first Council before launch.
2. Give standing to a named third party now — a co-op lawyer on
   retainer, fiscal sponsor, or designated community org.
3. Write the Council selection process. Founder-nominated + public

**Finding 4 — Covenant §3 and OA X.3 disagree.**

founder + 2/3 Council + 90-day notice. Post-member: 2/3 members.

**Finding 5 — 50,000-user trigger is defined by the company.**
Move "Verified Contribution" definition into Article X. Minimum:
published review, claimed listing, or completed help match. Changing
the definition requires the same supermajority as amending Article X.

**Finding 6 — $500k trigger is bookkeeping.**
Define revenue as consolidated gross receipts of the Company and any
affiliate under common control. Quarterly reporting against the line.

**Finding 7 — 90 days to convert is a built-in breach.**
90 days to file documents. 12 months to close. Specific performance
attaches to filing.

**Finding 8 — Acquisition language is a current-Jason promise.**
Ban asset sales, exclusive brand licenses, and acqui-hires that move
community data without assuming Article X. Publish in Exhibit A.

**Finding 12 — "Never sell your data" vs help form.**
One paragraph in Covenant §3. Same sentence in the form, confirmation
email, provider email.

**Finding 13 — Public moderation log vs retaliation.**
Publish reason codes and counts, not identities, until a process
exists.

**Finding 15 — "Binding commitment" is marketing until filed.**
Footer line changes. Emergent already notified (M-011).

## Accepted With Reservations

**Finding 9 — Investor acknowledgment will be laughed out of term
sheets.**
True. Most capital refuses equity-conversion clauses. But we say so out
loud in the covenant: "We may stay small. We may not take VC. If we
cannot fund the co-op, we still convert the governance even if the bank
account is ugly."

**Finding 10 — Surplus-by-usage can pay the parks, not the reviewers.**
Real risk. Fix is to design patronage math now. Split into reviewer
pool and payer pool. Percentages locked in Exhibit A.

**Finding 11 — Business members vote on their own ranking rules.**
Keep one-member-one-vote but bar any member with a claimed business
listing from voting on ranking-algorithm and ad-policy changes
specifically. Not on everything — just on those.

**Finding 14 — "Rough financials" is how transparency dies.**
Three numbers every quarter: gross receipts vs $500k, user count vs
50k, and whether any affiliate received platform-related revenue.
