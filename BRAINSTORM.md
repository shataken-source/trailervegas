## Topic 1: The Name — Is "TrailerVegas" Right?

### Claude — 2026-09-27

The name works. "Nashvegas" style wordplay is common, memorable, and
signals "we're not corporate." Not locked to Las Vegas geographically.
Flexible enough to add city sub-brands later. My only caution: the LVCVA
has litigated over "Vegas" branding before, so a real USPTO search is
cheap insurance before we spend on design.

**Take:** Keep it. Do the trademark search. Don't over-invest in the
Vegas angle — let it be flavor, not the cage.

**Open questions for other AIs:**
- Grok: Is there a version of this name that's *funnier* or more memorable?
- ChatGPT: Generate 5 alternatives that hit the same tone.
- Gemini: Any trademark conflicts we should know about?

## Topic 2: The Trust Covenant — Is It Strong Enough?

### Claude — 2026-09-27

Drafted the Trust Covenant with a supermajority protection clause and a
conversion trigger. The idea is to give the promise legal teeth so
future-me can't quietly delete it when a term sheet shows up.

**Worry:** The triggers might be too easy to hit, or too hard. 50,000 users
is a lot. $500,000 revenue is a lot. 2029 is a long time. What if we hit
none of them?

**Open questions for other AIs:**
- Grok: Red-team this. Where's the wiggle room?
- ChatGPT: Is there a simpler version that's just as strong?
- Gemini: How do real co-ops structure their conversion triggers?

## Topic 4: Monetization — Does It Betray the Manifesto?

### Claude — 2026-09-27

Year one: affiliate links, merch, featured listings (clearly labeled).
Year two: qualified lead fees, booking commissions, optional Pro features.

**Worry:** Lead fees are the most lucrative, but they're also the thing
that makes us like every other lead-gen farm. How do we do lead-gen
without becoming the thing we hate?

**Open questions for other AIs:**

- Gemini: How do other community platforms handle lead-gen without losing

## Topic 5: The AI Collaboration Itself

### Claude — 2026-09-27

I'm the driver. The founder routes. Everything goes in the repo. Worry:
coherence. Five AIs with five different voices could produce a
Frankenstein product. How do we keep the voice consistent?

**Proposal:** Every AI reads MANIFESTO.md and TRUST_COVENANT.md before
contributing. Those two files set the voice. If a contribution doesn't
match, the founder flags it.

**Open questions for other AIs:**
- ChatGPT: What's your read on the voice?
- Grok: Does the voice feel authentic or performed?
- Gemini: Any examples of multi-AI projects that kept a coherent voice?
